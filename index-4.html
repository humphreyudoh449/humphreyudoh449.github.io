Pilot studySurvey of Nigerian SME owners, September 2026

I designed a questionnaire, collected responses from owners and managers of Nigerian small businesses, and analysed what they use, what they gain, and what stops them. The goal was to find the practical problem that better analytics could solve.

34valid owners and managers
73.5%rely mainly on WhatsApp
50%credit it with over a quarter of sales
17.6%cannot estimate platform sales

The gap: value is felt, but not measured

Owners rate every benefit above neutral. The two lowest scores are the ability to attribute more customer enquiries to the platform (3.76 out of 5) and the ease of measuring results (3.79). Six owners could not say what share of sales came from their main platform at all. Respondents who posted daily scored higher on sales impact (4.50 against 3.55), which is a pattern worth testing in a larger study, not a proven effect.

What gets in the way

Biggest challenge in the owner's own words, coded to one theme per answer (32 answers).

Data cost, unstable power and network access, and limited reach were named most often. Three owners asked directly for AI or planning support, and others asked for cheaper data and better advertising features. Any tool for this market has to work on low bandwidth and low cost.

What this study cannot tell you

  • It is a pilot with a small convenience sample, so it shows patterns, not population estimates.
  • About a third of respondents are in Rivers State and 59% run the business alone.
  • All impact scores are self-reported opinions, each measured with a single question.
  • Only aggregate results are published here. No respondent details are shown.

Data preprocessing pipelinePython and Pandas, 2025

Before any model or report, the data has to be trustworthy. I built a reproducible workflow that checks whether a dataset is complete, consistent and ready for analysis.

  • Assessed completeness, consistency and integrity, with anomaly checks before modelling.
  • Used summaries, filtering, grouping and transformation to find performance differences across segments.

The same habit shaped my pilot study, where I documented every data-quality issue and how I handled it.

Research directionPlanned PhD proposal

Small businesses already hold the raw material for better decisions: thousands of customer messages, comments and orders. Most of it is informal, short and never analysed.

My proposed research develops low-cost methods that turn that text into structured insight, for example how customers feel about price, delivery or service quality. It has to run on modest devices and weak connections, and it has to respect that customer conversations are private.

  • Aspect-level sentiment extraction for informal text.
  • Pattern discovery, customer segmentation and anomaly detection.
  • Privacy-conscious design with data ownership kept with the people it belongs to.
